# Bery Brand

## Vision

**Everyone you meet, remembered.**

Bery is the personal memory layer for your relationships. You meet more people
than any mind can hold — Bery captures them in seconds and recalls them the
moment it matters. Not a sales pipeline, not a follow-up cadence tool: a second
brain for people, built around intent — *"who do I know that can help with
this?"* — so every relationship gets the attention it deserves.

## Personality

Warm, literary, quietly confident. Bery feels like a well-kept paper notebook,
not a SaaS dashboard. Lowercase wordmark, serif voice, one strawberry accent.

## Colors

Light is the brand default; dark is warm charcoal, never gray.

| Token | Hex | HSL | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cream Paper | `#F9F6F0` | `40 45% 96%` | Background (default) |
| Ink | `#1C1512` | `20 20% 9%` | Text, leaf, primary |
| Strawberry | `#CE2241` | `349 72% 47%` | Accent, berry body, the "." |
| Seed Cream | `#FBF8F4` | `40 45% 97%` | Seeds on berry, text on strawberry |
| Warm Charcoal | `#141110` | `24 12% 7%` | Dark-mode background |
| Strawberry Bright | `#E74059` | `351 78% 58%` | Accent on dark backgrounds |

Rules:
- Strawberry is the only decorative color. Everything else is paper and ink.
- On dark backgrounds use Strawberry Bright, never the light-mode Strawberry.
- The berry mark keeps cream seeds in both themes.

## Typography

- **Wordmark / display:** Fraunces, semibold (600), lowercase, tight tracking (-0.03em)
- **UI / body:** Geist
- The wordmark is always `bery` lowercase with a Strawberry period: **bery.**
